## Recommendation: NO-GO

The API is **not ready to handle real money**. 4 Critical defects are open, and any one of them blocks the release of a banking API: money can be created by concurrent payments, anyone can read and move any customer's money without logging in, and negative or oversized amounts are accepted.

## What was tested

- **Tested:** all 9 in-scope endpoint groups (login, customer, accounts, transactions and filters, transfer, deposit, withdraw, bill pay, loan) with 47 designed cases: positive, negative, boundary, security and data integrity; a 16-step customer journey; web UI cross-checks; 4 manual cases; 5 coded tests; a small k6 load test of `GET /accounts/{id}` and `POST /transfer`.
- **Not tested:** load on deposit, withdraw and bill pay; injection (blocked by the site firewall); UI functionality beyond cross-checks; admin endpoints and other customers' data (excluded by the ground rules).

## Risks

- **Financial integrity:** lost updates under concurrency and unvalidated amounts can create or destroy money; the books would not reconcile.
- **Security:** no authentication, and the password travels in the URL, so it ends up in proxy and server logs.
- **Availability of customer data:** one sub-cent transfer makes a customer's accounts unreadable (BUG-001).
- **Test limits:** a shared public sandbox (rate limit, periodic resets) allowed only small load; results on production-like hardware may differ.

## What would change the decision

1. Fix the Critical defects and the P1 High defects.
2. Re-run the full cycle (`./run.sh cycle`): journey and every P1 case pass, and the load test reconciles to the cent.
3. R&D confirms the proposed business rules and answers the spec questions.
